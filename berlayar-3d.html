Footprints are manually traced from the HDB June 2026 sales brochure, site plan. Block storey counts follow its published schedule. Top view retains the HDB plan and its annotations. The 3D view uses modelled ground, roads and greenery, with the highway geometry and Reflections tower profiles shared with the unit walkthrough. The printed scale ruler and notes below the site drawing are cropped out. Four future bus-stop markers are traced approximately from the plan’s bus-stop / shelter annotations; they are planned facilities, not currently operating stops.

This is a simplified planning model, not an architectural digital twin. Footprints are approximate tracings; heights use an illustrative 3 metres per storey. Facade joints and floor bands are illustrative visual details. Roof and facade details are illustrative. Regional terrain uses Mapzen Terrain Tiles sampled on 21 Nov 2026, coarsely aligned and levelled around the precinct. It is landscape context and is not used to calculate flat sightlines. Highway height, lane markings and the Reflections skybridges are approximate. Sunlight direction, wall brightness and ground shadows respond to the selected date and time. Shadows project simplified block heights onto flat ground; terrain, exact facades and shadows falling on other buildings are not calculated. It cannot establish whether a particular unit's view will be blocked.

Future sites follow HDB plan annotations and linked URA location maps. The Berlayar Drive condo marker is south of the estate, across Berlayar Drive and west of Bukit Chermin Road towards Berlayar Creek. No invented future towers are shown: exact footprints and heights require published designs. Reserved sites may extend beyond this plan's visible area. Turning off future markers does not remove printed annotations from the HDB ground layer.

URA lists a 5-storey maximum for the Berlayar Drive GLS site in its 15 May 2026 land-parcel schedule. That restriction is not a confirmed building design and is not used to invent a condo footprint here. All planned developments remain subject to changes and approval.

Sun bearing and elevation use the approximate NOAA solar-position equations, evaluated near Berlayar Rise (1.272° N, 103.808° E) in Singapore time. The compass and sunlight arrow follow the camera rotation. Ground shadows project opposite the solar bearing. Atmospheric refraction, terrain and facade-to-facade shading are excluded; shadow length follows height / tan(solar elevation), with longer, softer edges at low sun angles and a gradual fade as the sun reaches the horizon.
