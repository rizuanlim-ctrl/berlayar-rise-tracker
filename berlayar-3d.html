Sources, model accuracy & future developments

Footprints are manually traced from the HDB June 2026 sales brochure, site plan. Block storey counts follow its published schedule. The map uses the same plan as its ground layer, preserving roads, paths, gardens and site annotations. The printed scale ruler and notes below the site drawing are cropped out. Four future bus-stop markers are traced approximately from the plan’s bus-stop / shelter annotations; they are planned facilities, not currently operating stops.

This is a simplified planning model, not an architectural digital twin. Footprints are approximate tracings; heights use an illustrative 3 metres per storey. Facade joints and floor bands are illustrative visual details. Roof structures, terrain, setbacks and window views are not surveyed or modelled. Soft shadows are decorative studio lighting and are not a sun or shading simulation. It cannot establish whether a particular unit's view will be blocked.

Future sites follow HDB plan annotations and linked URA location maps. The Berlayar Drive condo marker is south of the estate, across Berlayar Drive and west of Bukit Chermin Road towards Berlayar Creek. No invented future towers are shown: exact footprints and heights require published designs. Reserved sites may extend beyond this plan's visible area. Turning off future markers does not remove printed annotations from the HDB ground layer.

URA lists a 5-storey maximum for the Berlayar Drive GLS site in its 15 May 2026 land-parcel schedule. That restriction is not a confirmed building design and is not used to invent a condo footprint here. All planned developments remain subject to changes and approval.

Sun bearing and elevation use the approximate NOAA solar-position equations, evaluated near Berlayar Rise (1.272° N, 103.808° E) in Singapore time. The compass is fixed to north; it does not rotate with the camera. Atmospheric refraction, terrain and building occlusion are excluded.

Coverage includes Berlayar Rise, Berlayar Residences (U/C) and Reflections at Keppel Bay. Residences footprints are traced from HDB’s October 2025 brochure. Nearby roads and Reflections footprints © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Geographic alignment is approximate, anchored to Telok Blangah MRT. Reflections is cropped to whole buildings with centres within 35° either side of south, measured from the approximate centre of Berlayar Rise (plan coordinates 485,435). This horizontal bearing filter is independent of camera rotation and does not model individual windows, occlusion or vertical viewing angles. Reflections uses simplified extrusions of mapped footprints; its distinctive curved roofs and façades are not reproduced. The developer describes six 24–41-storey towers and eleven villas. Upcoming condominium status follows URA’s September 2026 schedule; Drive and Close location markers do not represent exact parcel boundaries. Source checked 4 October 2026.
