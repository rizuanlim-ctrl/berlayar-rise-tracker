Sources, model accuracy & future developments

Footprints are manually traced from the HDB June 2026 sales brochure, site plan. Block storey counts follow its published schedule. The map uses the same plan as its ground layer, preserving roads, paths, gardens and site annotations.

This is a simplified planning model, not an architectural digital twin. Footprints are approximate tracings; heights use an illustrative 3 metres per storey. Facades, roof structures, terrain, setbacks and window views are not surveyed or modelled. It cannot establish whether a particular unit's view will be blocked.

Future sites are marked where the HDB plan identifies them. No invented future towers are shown: exact footprints and heights require published designs. Reserved sites may extend beyond this plan's visible area. Turning off future markers does not remove printed annotations from the HDB ground layer.

URA lists a 5-storey maximum for the Berlayar Drive GLS site in its 15 May 2026 land-parcel schedule. That restriction is not a confirmed building design and is not used to invent a condo footprint here. All planned developments remain subject to changes and approval.

Coverage: Berlayar Rise and the immediate vicinity shown on HDB's plan. Buildings farther away, including Reflections at Keppel Bay, are not modelled. Source checked 4 October 2026.
