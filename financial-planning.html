How the ratings are calculated

Insufficient Funds means the baseline runs short at booking, Agreement for Lease, keys or setup. Stretched means the conservative case runs short, assessed MSR reaches 25%, monthly cash remaining goes below zero, or cash left after setup is below the greater of S$1,000 and 10% of the purchase cash budget.

Comfortable requires MSR of 20% or less and monthly cash remaining of at least 15% of take-home income in the baseline and 10% in the conservative case. Other funded cases are Manageable. These are planner thresholds. The price ceiling uses the entered cash, CPF, loan limits and costs. It can assume a smaller loan if needed to fit monthly cash flow. Enter take-home pay and monthly expenses to include a monthly cash-flow check. Official HDB, CPF and lender assessments take precedence.

Projection assumptions

CPF credits are modeled monthly, with annual contribution growth and 2.5% illustrative OA interest modeled monthly. Actual CPF interest is computed on monthly lowest balances and credited annually; bonus allocation, salary ceiling, age, wage changes and grants can change the result. The planner's model is therefore an approximation, not a CPF statement.

Financial checks

How the figures are calculated

Booking fee is cash, credited to the downpayment. AFL requires the selected downpayment less booking fee, plus buyer's stamp duty and estimated legal fees. Available grant / OA is applied to the AFL amount. At keys, the remaining flat price is funded by an eligible HDB loan (subject to the 75% ceiling), available OA above the selected reserve and cash. One-off fees and extras are added separately. The mortgage payment is estimated using the amortisation formula at your chosen rate and term.
