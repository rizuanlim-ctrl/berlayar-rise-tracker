Rules checked on 26 November 2026 for the June 2026 BTO exercise. Official HDB, CPF Board, MAS-regulated lender and IRAS assessments take precedence. The planner estimates purchase funding using entered approvals, balances, expenses, dates and cash commitments.

## Assumptions and limits

- The price ceiling is an estimate for the entered purchase budget and financing terms. It does not determine HFE or bank eligibility, income exclusions or variable-income haircuts, treatment of existing properties or loan reassessment. Enter gross income as assessed by the lender and use actual HFE or bank terms. For special or subsequent properties, use CPF's housing-usage limit and the available OA after retirement set-asides.
- Starting OA and monthly OA credits come from the user. The forecast assumes monthly credit at the start of each modeled month and annual extra credits in December. It uses full-rate bands to scale entered credits rather than rebuilding payroll. Wage-ceiling changes, future statutory changes, CPF extra interest, interest accrued before the reference date, and overflow from fully funded RA or MediSave require user adjustments. The conservative case freezes growth, removes annual AWS/PB OA credits and starts with 20% less future regular OA. The reduction is editable.
- Age-55 transfer uses the primary buyer's SA/FRS against pooled household OA. Household cases with separate CPF age-55 events, uneven balances, individual retention limits or restrictions should use CPF's approved amounts. The planner does not model part-year grant apportionment or approvals. Unknown primary pre-key transfers suppress the price ceilings.
- Fees, insurance, cash surcharges and utilities costs remain editable estimates. Enter ABSD, bank legal fees, HPS premium and levy where applicable. Fire premiums after August 2029 and actual 2031 handover charges may differ. Mortgage payments use a level rate; refinancing, interest changes and accelerated repayments require separate calculations.
- Prime cash proceeds assume an eligible resale after the occupancy requirement is met. Confirm actual MOP, CPF refund and sale disbursements. Future value, transaction costs and tax are estimates.
- Saved tracker prices are indicative. Entries with evidently truncated prices below S$10,000 require a verified price. Short-lease modeling requires a separate HDB-confirmed price and CPF limit.
- Financial inputs are held in browser memory. A page-scoped content policy blocks network connections, form submissions and third-party resources. Only theme and active-room preferences are stored automatically. Excel export is an explicit download containing the entered financial values.
